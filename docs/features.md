# Product capabilities

## Public experience

- Responsive content and service pages
- Travel blog with article and category routes
- Clean URLs and breadcrumb navigation
- Newsletter acquisition flow
- Home Finder service presentation
- Remote-worker digital kit presentation and checkout
- Success, pending, and error return states for payments
- Privacy, terms, thank-you, and error pages

## Editorial experience

- Invitation-only content management
- Page, navigation, media, SEO, and blog editing
- Editorial review before publication
- Article preview with reusable content components
- Compatibility with migrated and current article formats
- Non-destructive content updates

## Search foundations

- Canonical URLs
- XML sitemap generation
- Robots directives
- Page-specific titles and descriptions
- Structured data for organizations, people, services, products, articles, and breadcrumbs where applicable
- Redirects from supported legacy URL formats
- Article publication and update dates

## Commerce and delivery

- Mercado Pago checkout initiation
- Payment return states
- Webhook-based verification and digital delivery
- Private storage for paid files
- Environment-based operational configuration

## Quality and privacy

- Automated tests for build, content, editorial preview, SEO, links, images, and serverless behavior
- Removal of embedded image metadata from public assets
- No credentials or customer records in public source
- Separation between production source and this public case study
